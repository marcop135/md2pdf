For agents

Public discovery and control notes for Markdown to PDF — a client-only converter at md2pdf.marcopontili.com.

How to use the app

  1. Open the live app (or local Vite on port 5173).
  2. Wait until window.md2pdf exists.
  3. window.md2pdf.setMarkdown(markdown) — string, max 2 MB.
  4. await window.md2pdf.prepareExport() — waits for preview and Mermaid, then applies the print filename hint.
  5. Prefer CDP Page.printToPDF with printBackground: true and preferCSSPageSize: true. Fallback: await window.md2pdf.exportPdf() (system print dialog).

Coding-agent procedure: md2pdf-export skill.
